Keeping Your Cherry Valley Water Heater Running Longer

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a water heater that runs for years and one that fails early. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode rod check every three to five years, a quick relief valve test once a year, a thermostat set near 120 degrees, and yearly descaling for any tankless unit on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically last 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms like rusty hot water, rumbling during heating, dampness under the tank, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one with water on the floor.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A sediment layer that's just starting to rumble needs only a flush, yet that same buildup, neglected through a few more years, can create hot spots on the tank floor and shorten the life of the tank, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number on your phone today so you can reach us fast as soon as something sounds or looks wrong. A quick call saves time, money, and cold showers.
